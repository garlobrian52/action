A History of Firsts

Tradeweb was the first electronic platform to offer global investors access to the Chinese bond market via Northbound Bond Connect, as well as the first trading platform to deliver the first fully electronic request-for-quote (RFQ) access via China Interbank Bond Market (CIBM) Direct Link – another popular route for overseas investors connecting to and trading in the Chinese onshore bond market. Following shortly thereafter, Tradeweb was one of the first platforms to launch Southbound Bond Connect.

Tradeweb again participated in the development of the world’s first derivatives market access scheme: Swap Connect. Through this new electronic trading channel, which aims to internationalize CNY IRS, offshore investors can trade and central counterparty (CCP) clear onshore CNY IRS without changing their existing trading and settlement practices.

As we continuously look at ways to innovate and expand access to new global markets, we’re helping clients discover improved liquidity, streamline their workflow, and have transparency all in one place.
